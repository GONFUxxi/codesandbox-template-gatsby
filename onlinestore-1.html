Explore our wide range of Scuba gear services.

  • Underwater gear

    Ideal for diving in temperate or warm waters. This suit allows a thin layer of water to remain between the suit and the body, which is warmed by body heat. It provides insulation but is not suitable for cold waters.

    Buy Underwater Gear Now

  • Drysuit

    Perfect for diving in cold waters. Unlike a wetsuit, a drysuit keeps the diver completely dry, thanks to seals at the wrists and neck. It is ideal for prolonged dives in cold conditions.

    Buy Drysuit

  • Neoprene Suit

    This can be either a wetsuit or a drysuit, depending on its design. Neoprene suits are known for their versatility and provide good thermal protection across a wide range of temperatures.

    Buy Neoprene Suit

  • Survival Suit

    Primarily used in commercial diving or extreme situations, this suit is designed to provide buoyancy and warmth in case of an emergency in cold water.

    Buy Survival Suit

  • Deep Diving Suit

    These suits are designed for deep dives where water pressure is substantial. They are often rigid and made to withstand extreme pressure conditions.

    Buy Deep Diving Suit

  • Cave Diving Suit

    These suits are specific for cave diving, where there is a high risk of scrapes and cuts. They are typically more durable and designed to protect the diver in tight and rocky environments.
